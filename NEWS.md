# PICOTsize 0.1.0

* Initial release.
* Sample size calculators for cross-sectional (binary and
  continuous), case-control, cohort, clinical trial (superiority,
  non-inferiority, equivalence), and diagnostic accuracy study
  designs, wrapping epiR as the validated computational backend.
* Shiny app with a PICOT-based design wizard, dynamic parameter
  forms, an interactive sensitivity plot, auto-generated
  Methods-section report text, and a Validation tab comparing
  results against Bhardwaj et al. (2024).
* Five documented methodological corrections relative to the
  worked examples in Bhardwaj et al. (2024): dropout formula,
  rounding convention, trial sidedness, and diagnostic test sample
  size pooling.
